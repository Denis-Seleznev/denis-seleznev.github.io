Happy Farm 3D

I decided to make the next project in a first person 3D environment. As an inspiration I've picked the famous classic game called Farm Frenzy.

Difficulties: Surprisingly, switching to 3D scene was not that hard. The only difficulties were connected with depicting warehouse contents, which was solved by establishing an additional List<>, containing the IDs of the products, and with creating a functional shop menu and synchronizing its UI with animals present in the scene and products stored at the warehouse.
